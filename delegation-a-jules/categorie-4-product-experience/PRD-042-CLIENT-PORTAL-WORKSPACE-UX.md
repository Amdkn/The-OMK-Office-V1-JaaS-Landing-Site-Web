# PRD-042 — Client Portal & Workspace UX
## Objective
Provide a coherent client portal around existing Micro-SaaS workspace archetypes.
## Scope
Workspace home, active services, recent sessions, status surfaces, resume actions, shell responsiveness.
## Contracts
Consume workspace shell/plugin contract, session persistence and specialization registry.

Own portal composition/UI only; archetype engines remain Category 1/2 territory.
## Acceptance
Multiple services remain isolated; resume works; legal/finance/career archetypes render through registry; loading/error/empty states tested; responsive keyboard navigation.
