# PRD-041 — 75-Service Discovery & Navigation
## Objective
Make the canonical 75-service catalogue discoverable without duplicating catalogue data.
## Scope
Search, pole/category filtering, service cards, deep links, empty/no-result states, service-to-launcher transition.

Own discovery/navigation presentation and selectors. Never fork or hard-code the 75-service catalogue.
## Acceptance
All 75 services discoverable; deterministic filtering/search; deep links resolve; visitor/client routing preserved; responsive and accessible tests.
